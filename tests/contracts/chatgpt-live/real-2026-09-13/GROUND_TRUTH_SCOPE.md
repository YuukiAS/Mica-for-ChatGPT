# Real Contract Pack Scope

This pack is canonical broad ChatGPT Live Contract evidence for the 2026-09-13 Atlas run. It proves sanitized semantic surfaces, lifecycle ordering, connector and composer surface presence, timing derivation, privacy sanitation, and replay coverage for the committed `real-2026-09-13` fixture.

It does not prove every exact native parent/sibling relationship inside the assistant action row. It also does not prove every connector send race where ChatGPT commits a new user turn while native virtualization keeps the mounted user-turn count unchanged.

A targeted live probe is warranted when a real page failure depends on exact action-cluster ancestry, sibling order, or commit identity semantics that the broad Atlas pack intentionally materialized or reconstructed after rejecting unsafe captures.

Targeted probes should enrich this pack with commit-safe lengths, anonymous hashes, geometry, generic labels, and selector invariants. They should not trigger another broad Round 1-6 Atlas campaign.
